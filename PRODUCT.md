## Platform
web

## Stack
Python, PyTorch, TorchVision, Streamlit, Matplotlib. Specified by the user.

## Users
Rakhman Seidigali, group SE-2411, Astana IT University. Course instructor: Nurym Kaiyrkhan.

## Product Purpose
WeatherVision classifies photographs as Cloudy, Rain, Shine, or Sunrise using three comparable trained models. The demo supports an honest academic comparison and an explanation of one prediction.

## Operating Context
Local browser demonstration; training in Google Colab or locally; submission due November 7, 2026, 23:59 Asia/Almaty.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Upload or camera input, image preview, model selection, four softmax scores, device and measured latency, ConvNeXt Grad-CAM, real experimental figures. Saved checkpoints only. CPU inference required. Missing checkpoints must be understandable. No invented results. The four categories are Cloudy, Rain, Shine, and Sunrise.

## Brand Commitments
WeatherVision; modern, minimal light theme, clear typography and consistent spacing. Professional dashboard, restrained decoration. English suitable for undergraduate students.

## Evidence on Hand
The dataset has been audited, all four experiments have been trained, and their checkpoints and evaluation outputs are stored locally.

## Product Principles
- Make the image and classification task easy to understand.
- Present the measured model comparison clearly.
- Keep comparison and explanation accessible during a short defense.
- Never imply weather forecasting or calibrated certainty.
